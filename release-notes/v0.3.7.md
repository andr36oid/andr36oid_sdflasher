you can now install hotfixes straight from the flasher. it checks the andr36oid hotfixes list for your card's exact build, ticks the bug fixes for you, leaves the optional extras unticked, and copies what you pick to the card. you can do it on its own or as a step right after flashing, and the card applies them the next time the console starts.

recovery is safer now. before it restores a backup it confirms the card is the same one the backup was taken from, by checking the parts of the card the backup kept. if it can't be sure, it stops before writing anything, so a backup can't land on the wrong card and overwrite it.

flashing over USB on android also copes better with readers that stall or hand back short transfers, instead of getting stuck or writing part of the data.
