## andr36oid SD Flasher v0.7.0

The flasher for the next andr36oid tester build. A new card can now get the game system of your choice, counterfeit cards are caught before anything is written, and when something goes wrong the flasher says what happened and what to do.

- Choose the game system for a new card: dArkOS, dArkOSRE, dArkOSen or none. The flasher downloads it, checks it and writes it to its own partition at the end of the card. dArkOS and dArkOSRE need the next tester build or newer; with older builds only dArkOSen is offered. Text mode takes the same choice with -game-system.
- Catches cards that are not the size they claim. Before an installation writes the release, a few markers are written across the card and read back. A counterfeit card stops there with a message that names the size it really holds, and nothing of the release is on it.
- Checks the free space on your computer before a download or an unpack, and tells you how much is needed.
- Clear messages when something fails. Instead of a bare system error such as "unexpected EOF" or "permission denied", the flasher now says what happened in plain words (the card reader stopped responding, the download was cut off, the image is damaged, GitHub is limiting requests, the computer's clock is wrong, and so on) and what to do next. It also says what the failure left the card like: nothing written, incomplete and to be written again, or put back as it was. The technical detail stays below the message, and a new "Copy details" button puts it on the clipboard for a bug report.
- After a failed write that points at the card or the reader, the flasher suggests trying another reader or computer, or on Android, that some phones cannot write cards this way.
- Downloads that break off or stall are resumed where they stopped, up to three times, instead of starting from zero or giving up after half an hour. A connection that sends nothing for a minute counts as stalled.
- Says plainly why an image that is a copy of a used card is refused: it could carry someone's personal data onto the new card.
- Keeps the computer awake while a card is written.
- A write that stops halfway because the helper was closed or the computer shut down now says that the card is incomplete and must be written again.
- An update that would need the space of the card's game system stops before writing and explains why.
- A card with its lock switch on is now listed, marked write-protected, instead of missing from the list. A USB hard drive or SSD asks once more, naming its size and model, before it is written. A card in a Mac's built-in reader is listed.

This flasher pairs with the next andr36oid tester build and still writes every andr36oid release from 22 October 2026 or later.
