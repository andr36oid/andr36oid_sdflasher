## andr36oid SD Flasher v0.6.0

The flasher for the releases that come as four images: kernel 4.19 or 5.10, each with the OpenGL ES or the Vulkan graphics driver. Older flashers cannot open these images.

- Opens the new images. Their kernel is packed as Image.gz and they also come as .img.gz; v0.5.1 stops on them with "missing or truncated Image".
- The release list shows the recommended image (5.10 with Vulkan) first and keeps the other three one step away.
- Partitions the card itself for releases that accept it: Android storage, the bundled apps and the EASYROMS games drive are in place straight after the flash, so the console's first start has nothing to move or resize.
- Can put dArkOSen on a new card. For releases that support it, the options page offers to download the dArkOSen release, check it and write it to its own partition at the end of the card. The Android app can do the same.
- Advanced: choose the bootloader for a card (the image's own, the standard one or the newer mainline one), with warnings for combinations known not to work, and update the device trees on a card without flashing it again. Both are in the window and in text mode (-bootloader, -boot-chain, -sleep-firmware, -action bootloader, -action dtbs, -undo).
- An update that keeps your data now checks first that the new image can read the card's Android storage.
- Fixes offered after a flash wait until the console has done its first setup.
- All new texts are translated into the ten languages, and the fonts cover the new characters.

This flasher pairs with the andr36oid four-image releases and still writes every andr36oid release from 2026-10-22 or later.
